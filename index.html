How I work

My design principles

01
Problem before pixels
I don't open Figma until I've understood the user's world and the business's goal. Research isn't a phase — it's how every conversation starts. I've run contextual interviews from Russia to Chicago to get there.
02
Complexity is an invitation
Enterprise UX means ambiguous briefs, legacy constraints, and a dozen opinions in the room. I've learned to love it — that friction is exactly where real design thinking earns its keep.
03
Design is a team sport
My best outcomes came from pulling the right voices in early. I facilitate, not just design — workshops, critiques, cross-functional alignment. I also mentor juniors because better designers make better products.
04
Ship, learn, iterate
I've built things that launched and are in daily use. From J&J's ALM revamp to Accenture's global AI platforms — I design with production reality in mind. Getting something live is part of the craft.

01
Rhythm

I practice Bharatanatyam.

Long before I learned to design, I learned to tell stories with my body. Bharatanatyam taught me discipline, expression, and the quiet power of detail — the way a single mudra can change a meaning, the way rhythm holds emotion in place.

It's where I first understood that the smallest things matter most. Every product I design still carries a little bit of that ethic.

🪔 Tradition💫 Expression🎵 Rhythm
02
Endurance

I'm a marathon girlie.

There's something about kilometre 28, when your body wants to stop and your mind has to take over, that changes how you think about anything difficult.

Running taught me that long projects — like long runs — aren't about sprints. They're about pace, breath, and showing up the next day too. Same energy, every complex multi-month design project.

🏃‍♀️ One step at a time⛰️ Showing up
03
Detail

I collect stamps.

Every stamp I own carries a story — a place, a moment, a tiny square of someone's world pressed into permanence. I started collecting as a kid, moving from city to city, and never stopped.

It's the same instinct that makes me a UX designer. Notice the small things. Save them. Find the story inside them. Use them later.

📮 Collector forever🔍 Detail-obsessed
04
Mood

Music is my thinking soundtrack.

I have a playlist for every mood, every project phase, every kind of day. Lo-fi for wireframes. Indie for research. Loud, defiant tracks for the moment a deadline gets real.

It's how I shift gears between thinking modes — and honestly, it's how I keep design joyful when the work gets hard.

🎧 Playlists for every phase🎵 Always on
05
Craft

I love to cook.

Cooking is design in disguise — a brief (the recipe), constraints (what's in the fridge), iteration (taste, adjust, taste again), and a delighted end user (whoever I'm feeding).

It's also one of the only places I'm patient with imperfection — every dish teaches me something the next one will use.

🍳 Always experimenting🌶️ Feeds the soul
06
Quiet

I read. A lot.

Fiction, non-fiction, design books, the occasional self-help when I need a nudge. Reading is where I refill — where I learn to see things from someone else's chair.

It's also how I stay humble. There's always so much more to know. And the best user research happens when you forget you're a designer and just become a curious reader of the world.

📚 Always reading💭 Always learning
07
Calm

Pets, plants, and slow walks.

There's a part of me that just wants to sit with my plants and watch them grow. Or take long walks with a dog who doesn't care about deadlines.

Nature reminds me to slow down, notice, and trust the process — three things that have made me a calmer designer and a better collaborator. Urgency is overrated. Presence isn't.

🌿 Plant parent🐕 Dog person☀️ Unhurried
08
Speed

An F1 enthusiast.

There is something about Formula 1 that is pure UX — every tenth of a second matters, every decision under pressure is a trade-off, and the best teams win not just on speed but on strategy, communication, and preparation.

I love the sport for the same reasons I love design: the details that the audience never sees are exactly what make the outcome exceptional.

🏎️ Race weekends🏁 Strategy over speed
